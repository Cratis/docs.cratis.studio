What you can do

Section titled “What you can do”
Discover your domain

Brainstorm what happens in the business on sticky notes, then find the concepts, rules and people behind it.

Model on the board

Lay out screens, commands, events and read models on one timeline, and shape them into slices.

Design screens

Sketch the screens people will use, and preview them with realistic data and personas.

Check behavior

Pin down every rule with Given/When/Then examples, and play the model as a running application.

Work with AI

Tell the board what to change, get suggestions, and model by voice with your team’s AI provider.

Build and deliver

Follow every slice from idea to done, generate code, and hand slices to builders and coding agents.

Every example uses one shop

All the guides and pictures use Acme Commerce, a sample online shop in five parts: catalog, shop, sales, purchasing and warehouse. Once you know it, every page reads like the next chapter of the same story.
